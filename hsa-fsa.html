How payment works

District Medical Rentals LLC accepts standard payment methods for all rentals. Our medical equipment rentals may qualify for HSA/FSA reimbursement when medically necessary.

Upon payment, we provide an itemized receipt that includes our business information, equipment details, rental dates, and payment confirmation. You can submit this documentation directly to your HSA/FSA administrator for reimbursement consideration.

Eligibility and reimbursement are determined by your individual plan administrator. A letter of medical necessity from your clinician is often what the plan asks for alongside the receipt.

What the receipt includes

  • Business information

    District Medical Rentals LLC, so your administrator can see who was paid.

  • Equipment details

    The lift recliner, hospital bed, sit-to-stand lift, or combination you rented.

  • Rental dates

    The period the equipment was in the home.

  • Payment confirmation

    Proof the rental was paid, which most HSA and FSA claims require.

Common questions

Do you bill insurance directly?

We take payment for the rental and give you an itemized receipt. You submit that receipt to your HSA, FSA, or other plan administrator. We do not decide whether a plan will reimburse you.

Can a lift chair rental be paid with HSA or FSA funds?

Many administrators reimburse medically necessary durable medical equipment, including a lift recliner, when you have a receipt and clinical documentation. Your plan makes that decision.

What about a hospital bed or sit-to-stand lift?

The same process applies. Tell us what you are renting and we will itemize it on the receipt with the rental dates and payment confirmation.
