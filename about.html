About us

We believe post-surgery recovery equipment shouldn’t cost an arm and a leg. Rent clean, medical-grade support for home—use it while you heal, return it when you’re done.

Who we are

District Medical Rentals is a local team serving Virginia, Maryland, and Washington, DC. We deliver medical-grade lift chairs and electronic medical beds to people recovering after surgery, caregivers helping loved ones, and anyone who needs safer, more comfortable mobility at home.

Healing is hard enough. Buying a pricey chair or bed for a few weeks of recovery shouldn’t be part of the stress. We make quality equipment easy to get, simple to use, and easy to send back.

What we believe

Comfort and safety at home should be affordable. Families shouldn’t have to choose between recovery support and a big upfront purchase. When you rent with us, you get the equipment you need for the time you need it—without paying full retail for something you’ll only use temporarily.
