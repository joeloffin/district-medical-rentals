Recliners and swelling in the legs

October 27, 2026

Swelling is common after leg and hip surgery and after long periods of sitting. A recliner that supports the legs lets you rest them up without a stack of pillows that roll away.

How high, and for how long, is a question for your surgeon or therapist. The chair simply holds the position they want. Do not stay in one posture all day. Short walks, as allowed, still matter.

If swelling suddenly increases, or a leg is hot and painful, call your clinician. Equipment is for comfort and positioning, not for diagnosing a problem.
