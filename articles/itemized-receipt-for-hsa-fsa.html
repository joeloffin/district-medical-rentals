What to send your HSA or FSA for a rental reimbursement

November 19, 2026

An HSA or FSA claim for a rental fails most often because the receipt is vague. “Medical equipment” with no dates and no seller name is not enough. Administrators want to see a real business, the item, the service period, and that you paid.

After you pay District Medical Rentals LLC, the itemized receipt includes our business information, the equipment details, the rental dates, and payment confirmation. That is the packet you can submit directly for reimbursement consideration.

Bring or upload whatever else your administrator lists. Common extras are a prescription, a letter of medical necessity, and the explanation of benefits if a health plan already processed part of the cost. We cannot write the clinical letter. Your surgeon, physician, or therapist does that.

Submit to the administrator, not to us. We can resend the receipt if you need a copy. We cannot tell you that a claim will be approved. Eligibility and reimbursement are determined by your individual plan administrator.

The rentals this applies to are our lift recliners, electronic hospital beds, and sit-to-stand patient lifts, delivered in DC, Maryland, and Virginia. Text or call 917-346-5309 before you book if you want the receipt worded for an HSA or FSA claim.
