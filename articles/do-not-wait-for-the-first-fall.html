Do not wait for the first fall

November 2, 2026

Families often call us after someone has already slid off a couch or spent a night stuck flat. The rental is more useful the day you come home, when energy is low and habits are not built yet.

If a discharge planner, therapist, or family member has already said “that couch is a problem,” believe them. Furniture does not become easier in the first week.

Text 917-346-5309 if the timeline is tight. We answer texts quickly and can often deliver the same day.
