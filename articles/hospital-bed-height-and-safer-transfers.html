Hospital bed height and safer transfers

October 25, 2026

Transfers fail when the heights do not match. Too low and you drop. Too high and you have to hop. An electronic bed raises and lowers so the edge lines up with a wheelchair, commode, or the lift recliner.

Lock the wheels, put the feet on the floor, and move one motion at a time: sit, pause, stand, pause, step. Rushing the pause is when people miss the chair.

Tell us if a wheelchair or bedside commode is part of the room. We will leave the bed where that path stays clear.

General information for home recovery, not medical advice. Follow the instructions from your surgeon or therapist. District Medical Rentals delivers medical-grade lift recliners and electronic hospital beds in Washington, DC, Maryland, and Virginia.
