Lift recliners after hip or knee surgery

October 24, 2026

Hip and knee precautions often limit how far you bend and how you twist. A deep sofa fights those rules. A lift recliner holds you in a supported sit and brings you forward when it is time to stand.

Use the chair for the seats you use most: meals, visits, and the hours you are awake. Keep the walker within reach before you start the lift, and let the chair finish its motion before you step away.

Your surgeon’s limits still come first. The chair supports those limits. It does not replace them.
