The first night home after surgery

October 26, 2026

Hospitals send people home tired. The equipment should not be a project that night. Have the bed or chair in place, plugged in, and tested before discharge.

Keep a light on for bathroom trips. Put the phone within reach. If a recliner is the sleep spot, which some surgeons prefer for a short time, make sure it reclines without trapping you too far back to stand.

If the plan changes and you are discharged early, call or text us. Same-day delivery is part of how we work.

General information for home recovery, not medical advice. Follow the instructions from your surgeon or therapist. District Medical Rentals delivers medical-grade lift recliners and electronic hospital beds in Washington, DC, Maryland, and Virginia.
