Using HSA and FSA funds for medical equipment rentals

November 19, 2026

A health savings account or flexible spending account is money set aside for qualified medical expenses. A rental is not a special category. Administrators look at whether the equipment is medically necessary, whether you have already paid, and whether the receipt shows who was paid, what was rented, and when.

District Medical Rentals LLC takes standard payment for the rental. After payment we give you an itemized receipt with our business information, the lift recliner, hospital bed, or sit-to-stand lift, the rental dates, and payment confirmation. You submit that documentation to your HSA or FSA administrator.

Some administrators also want a letter of medical necessity from the surgeon, physician, or therapist. Ask before delivery day so you are not waiting on a clinic signature after the claim window starts.

We do not decide eligibility. Your plan does. If they approve the claim, the reimbursement comes from them, not from us. If they deny it, the receipt is still a clear record of a medical equipment rental in the home.

Families in Washington, DC, Maryland, and Virginia can text 917-346-5309 and say they plan to use HSA or FSA funds. We will note the equipment and dates so the receipt matches what you tell the administrator. Read the full payment steps on our HSA and FSA page.
