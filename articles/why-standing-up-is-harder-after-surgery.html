Why standing up is harder after surgery

October 23, 2026

The first days home are often when standing feels hardest. A low couch or soft chair drops your hips below your knees, so your legs, back, and incision have to do extra work just to get you upright.

A medical-grade lift recliner raises and tilts you toward standing. You still do the last step yourself, but you are not hauling your full weight out of a deep seat. That matters after hip, knee, back, or abdominal surgery, and for anyone whose legs are simply tired.

Renting one for the weeks you need it is usually easier than buying a chair you will not use once you are walking well again.
