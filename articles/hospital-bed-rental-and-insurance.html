Hospital bed rentals and insurance reimbursement

November 19, 2026

An electronic hospital bed changes recovery at home because the surface raises the head and knees. People rent one after back surgery, joint replacement, abdominal surgery, or when breathing is easier upright. Search results for hospital bed rentals covered by insurance are full of promises. The useful version is narrower: some plans reimburse a bed rental, and they want proof.

Typical proof is a clinician’s order or letter, the diagnosis or procedure, and a paid invoice that names the bed and the dates it was in the home. District Medical Rentals LLC supplies the invoice side: business name, equipment details, rental dates, and payment confirmation.

Insurance rules vary. Original Medicare and Medicare Advantage have their own criteria for hospital beds, and private plans can be stricter or looser. Your administrator, not the rental company, says yes or no. HSA and FSA reimbursement is a separate path many families use when the health plan will not pay the vendor directly.

We deliver and set up the bed in DC, Maryland, and Virginia, including same-day and next-day, and pick it up when the recovery window ends. Rentals of four weeks or longer are discounted, and there is no deposit.
