Breathing easier with the head of the bed raised

October 27, 2026

Lying flat can make coughing, reflux, and shortness of breath worse. Raising the head of the bed keeps the chest higher than the stomach without relying on pillows that flatten by morning.

Start with a modest angle and adjust until rest feels easier. Your clinician may give you a target. The bed remembers nothing magic. It just holds the angle.

If breathing is suddenly worse, that is a medical issue, not a bed setting. Seek care. Use the bed for the recovery plan you already have.
