Sleeping positions a hospital bed can hold

October 24, 2026

Pillows slide. At 2 a.m. you end up flat, then struggle to rebuild the stack. A hospital bed keeps the angle you set. Head up for reflux or breathing, knees up so you do not inch toward the foot of the bed.

Side rails, when you want them, give a handhold for turning. The height adjustment lets you sit on the edge with both feet on the floor before you stand.

If you only need the head raised, say so when you book. We will still set the bed for the way you actually sleep.

General information for home recovery, not medical advice. Follow the instructions from your surgeon or therapist. District Medical Rentals delivers medical-grade lift recliners and electronic hospital beds in Washington, DC, Maryland, and Virginia.
