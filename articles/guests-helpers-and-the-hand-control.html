Guests, helpers, and the hand control

October 31, 2026

The person recovering may be medicated or half asleep when they need to move. The helper who knows the buttons is the one who prevents a scramble.

At delivery, have that helper in the room. Practice one full lift, or one head-up and head-down, while we are still there. Label nothing fancy. Just agree which button does what.

If a new helper arrives later in the rental, a two-minute practice in the daytime is worth more than instructions shouted at midnight.
