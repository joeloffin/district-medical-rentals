Set the house up before surgery day

October 24, 2026

The ride home from the hospital is a bad time to discover the couch is too low. We deliver same day and next day, including before the procedure, so the chair or bed is already in place.

Pick a spot with a clear path from the door, a nearby outlet, and enough room to walk with a walker or cane. Move throw rugs and low tables before delivery, not after.

Tell us about stairs, narrow hallways, and where you want the equipment. Setup is included in the delivery fee.
